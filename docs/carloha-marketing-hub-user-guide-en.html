Carloha Marketing Hub
User Guide

A practical guide for sales teams, dealers, and marketing support users. Learn how to find materials, open Google Drive folders, submit requests, and use official Carloha marketing assets correctly.

Audience Internal sales teams, dealers, marketing teams, and authorized users who need Carloha marketing materials.
Purpose Help users independently search, open, request, and use Carloha marketing resources.

1. What This Website Is For

Carloha Marketing Hub is the central access point for Carloha marketing resources. Files are hosted in Google Drive, while the website organizes them by vehicle, category, status, and update date so users can find the right materials faster.

Find Vehicle Materials Access materials for Tiggo 9, Himla, iCAUR V23, Tiggo 2 Pro, Tiggo 4, Tiggo 8 Pro, Arrizo 5, Chery Q, and other listed vehicles.
Open General Materials Find brand assets, logos, dealer guidelines, showroom materials, event materials, holiday campaigns, and PR articles.
Submit Requests Request missing materials, report broken links, ask product questions, or submit marketing execution questions.
Ask Carloha AI Use the assistant in the sidebar to ask where materials are, how to report issues, or how to submit a request.
Important: Materials are for authorized Carloha sales and dealer use only. Before external communication, confirm the latest version, vehicle specifications, pricing, and market suitability.

Recommended Workflow

  1. Open the website home page.
  2. Use the language toggle in the sidebar to choose English or Chinese.
  3. Search by vehicle name or material type from the home page.
  4. Open a search result, vehicle card, or material button.
  5. View or download the file in Google Drive.
  6. If a material is not available or a link does not work, submit a request from the Q&A / Request page.

3. How To Search For Materials

The home page search is the fastest way to find materials across the whole site. You can search by vehicle, material type, category, or status, then narrow results with filters.

Useful Search Examples

Tiggo 9 Himla iCAUR V23 Brochures Official Photos Training Materials Brand Assets Dealer Guidelines Ready Coming Soon

Ready and Coming Soon are status filters. Brochures, Official Photos, and similar labels are material types.

  1. Go to the Home page.
  2. Enter a vehicle name or material type in the Keyword search box.
  3. If there are too many results, use Vehicle / Category, Material Type, and Status filters.
  4. Review the title, vehicle/category, material type, status, and last updated date.
  5. Click Open to access the material link.
  6. Click Clear filters when you want to start over.
If you search for `Q3` or `QQ3`, the site treats it as `Chery Q` to help users find the right materials.

If You Cannot Find A Material

  • Try searching only the vehicle name instead of the full file title.
  • Try English material types such as Brochures, Official Photos, or Official Videos.
  • Clear filters in case the search is too narrow.
  • If the material is still missing, submit a New material request.

4. How To Use Vehicle Materials

The Vehicle Materials page organizes resources by vehicle. Each vehicle has a detail page with available and upcoming material types.

Steps

  1. Click Vehicle Materials in the sidebar.
  2. Search by vehicle name or material type at the top of the page.
  3. Click the vehicle card you need.
  4. On the vehicle detail page, review the image, last updated date, and material buttons.
  5. Select the needed material type, such as Specification Sheet, Brochures, Official Photos, Official Videos, or Training Materials.
  6. The material opens in Google Drive in a new tab.

Common Material Types

Material Type Typical Use
Specification Sheet Official specifications and product parameters.
Brochures Product introduction, customer communication, and sales preparation.
Flyers Short promotional materials for events, handouts, or local marketing.
Official Photos Official vehicle images for approved marketing and social media use.
Official Videos Official video assets.
Social Media Videos Video assets suitable for social media promotion.
Training Materials Sales training, dealer training, and internal learning.
If a Google Drive link does not open, it is usually a permission or link issue. Instead of repeatedly refreshing, submit a Broken link report from the Q&A / Request page.

5. How To Use General Materials

General Materials are resources that are not tied to one specific vehicle, such as brand assets, logos, dealer guidelines, event materials, and showroom files.

  1. Click General Materials in the sidebar.
  2. Search by category or keyword, such as Logo, Brand Assets, or Dealer Guidelines.
  3. Review the material card, status, and description.
  4. Click Open Folder.
  5. View or download the needed files in Google Drive.

6. Understanding Material Status

The material button tells you whether a resource can be opened immediately.

Status Meaning What To Do
Ready The material is available and normally has a Google Drive link. Click Open or the material button to access Google Drive.
Coming Soon The material is not available yet, or there is no active link. Click Coming Soon to open the request page with related information prefilled.

What To Do When You See Coming Soon

  1. Click the Coming Soon button.
  2. The site opens the Q&A / Request page.
  3. Check whether the prefilled vehicle and material type are correct.
  4. Add your name, email, market/dealer, purpose, and deadline.
  5. Submit the request and wait for the marketing team to review it.

7. How To Submit A Question Or Request

Use the Q&A / Request page when you need new materials, find a broken link, have a product material question, or need marketing execution support.

Which Request Type Should You Choose?

Request Type Use Case
New material request A material is missing, or a Coming Soon material is needed urgently.
Broken link report A Google Drive link does not open, has a permission issue, or points to the wrong file.
Product question You have a question about specifications, images, videos, or brochure content.
Marketing execution question You need support for events, showroom display, social media, or local promotion.
Other Your question does not fit the categories above.

Submission Steps

If you arrived from a Coming Soon button, some vehicle and material fields may already be prefilled. If you opened Q&A / Request from the sidebar, fill those fields manually when relevant.

  1. Click Q&A / Request in the sidebar.
  2. Select a Request Type.
  3. Select Urgency.
  4. Enter your Name and Email.
  5. Add WhatsApp, Market / Dealer, Vehicle, and Material Type when relevant.
  6. In Request Details, explain what you need, how it will be used, and whether there is a deadline.
  7. Click Submit Request.
A useful request includes purpose, target material, and deadline. Example: Nigeria dealer needs English Tiggo 9 social media videos for a Facebook post this Friday.

8. How To Use Carloha AI

Carloha AI is the assistant in the sidebar. It can use vehicle materials, general materials, and Carloha Wiki context to help you find information faster.

You Can Ask Questions Like

  • Which Tiggo 9 materials are available?
  • Where can I find Himla materials?
  • Where can I find iCAUR V23 videos?
  • The Google Drive link does not open. What should I do?
  • How can I request new materials?
  • Where can I find dealer guidelines?

Steps

  1. Click Carloha AI at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Select a suggested prompt or type your own question.
  3. Review the answer and any listed sources.
  4. If AI creates a request draft, check whether the details are correct.
  5. Complete the required fields in the draft card inside the AI sidebar, then confirm submission there.
Carloha AI is a support tool. For pricing, specifications, launch timing, and external commitments, always confirm with the latest official materials and the marketing team.

9. Usage Guidelines

Before downloading or sharing materials, review the Usage Guidelines page. The most important rules are:

  • Always use the latest version shown on the website.
  • Do not modify logos, brand visuals, or official product information without approval.
  • Confirm product specifications and prices before external sharing.
  • Dealers may use approved materials for local marketing and social media promotion.
  • If you are not sure whether a material can be used externally, submit a request or contact the marketing team first.

Checklist Before Use

Check What To Confirm
Version The material is the latest version shown on the website.
Market The material is suitable for your country, region, or dealer use case.
Content Vehicle specs, pricing, images, videos, and selling points are accurate.
Authorization The material is approved for external sharing or social media use.

10. Quick Troubleshooting

I Cannot Find A Material

  • Search by vehicle name from the Home page.
  • Check Vehicle Materials or General Materials.
  • Clear filters and search again.
  • If it is still missing, submit a New material request.

A Google Drive Link Does Not Open

  • Make sure you are using a Google account with access permission.
  • Check whether your network can access Google Drive.
  • Submit a Broken link report from Q&A / Request.

A Material Shows Coming Soon

  • This means the material is not available yet or has no active link.
  • Click Coming Soon and submit a request for the marketing team to follow up.

The Request Form Does Not Submit

  • Check required fields: name, email, request type, and request details.
  • Make sure the email format is valid.
  • If you submitted many times quickly, wait a moment and try again.
